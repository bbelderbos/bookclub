## This week's reading

**The Basic Tools** (7 topics)

- The Power of Plain Text
- Shell Games
- Power Editing
- Version Control
- Debugging
- Text Manipulation
- Engineering Daybooks

By the end of this week you'll be able to:

- Explain why plain text outlives the tools that produce it
- Automate a repetitive task with the shell instead of clicking through it
- Name the editor skills worth drilling until they're reflexes
- Debug by reproducing first and fixing second, without blaming the compiler
- Keep an engineering daybook and use it to remember decisions

## Put it into practice

**Editor drill (20 min):** Watch yourself edit for ten minutes and note every time you reach for the mouse or repeat a keystroke. Look up the shortcut for the two most common ones and use only those for a week.

**Daybook (5 min a day):** Keep a plain text file or notebook this week. Write down what you tried, what failed, and why you chose what you did. Share one entry that saved you time in the Slack thread.
