## This week's reading

**Before the Project** (4 topics)

- The Requirements Pit
- Solving Impossible Puzzles
- Working Together
- The Essence of Agility

By the end of this week you'll be able to:

- Treat requirements as something you discover through feedback, not something handed to you
- Separate real constraints from assumed ones when a problem seems impossible
- Weigh pairing and mob programming against working solo
- Explain agility as a loop (see where you are, take a small step, adjust), not a process to follow

## Put it into practice

**Requirement to problem (20 min):** Take a ticket from your backlog. Rewrite it as the problem the user has, without naming a solution. Ask the requester whether you got it right.

**Pair or mob session (60 min):** Pick a task you'd normally do alone and do it with one or more teammates at one keyboard. Afterwards, note what went faster, what went slower, and what you learned.
