## This week's reading

**Pragmatic Projects** (5 topics)

- Pragmatic Teams
- Coconuts Don’t Cut It
- Pragmatic Starter Kit
- Delight Your Users
- Pride and Prejudice

By the end of this week you'll be able to:

- Apply the book's individual habits (no broken windows, DRY, small steps) at team level
- Tell a practice that works for your context apart from a cargo cult copied from someone else's
- Name the starter kit every project needs: version control, relentless testing, full automation
- Measure success by the value users get, not by features delivered
- Sign your work: take ownership of it and let others see it

## Put it into practice

**Automation gap (30 min):** Write down every manual step between a merged change and running in production. Automate the smallest one, or file it with an owner.

**Retro on the book (20 min):** List the three ideas from this book you'll keep using. Post them in the Slack thread and say which one you'll bring to your team first.
